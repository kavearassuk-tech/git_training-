RMS TITANIC

A ship that became history.

RMS Titanic was a British ocean liner operated by the White Star Line. The ship departed Southampton on April 10, 1912, on her maiden voyage to New York.

Today, Titanic remains an important subject for maritime engineering, underwater archaeology, robotics and deep-sea exploration.
